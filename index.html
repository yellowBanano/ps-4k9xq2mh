Polaroid Sheets

Style for every card

Pick photos and they're laid out two to a 4×6 sheet, ready to print and cut.

Photos never leave this phone, and nothing is kept once you close the app.

To move this photo, tap any other card.

Print

  • Load 4 × 6 in (10 × 15 cm) photo paper.
  • If the print options show a paper size, pick 4 × 6.
  • Cut down the middle, between the grey ticks.

Printing tips

  • The sheet is exactly 4 × 6 in, so print it at full size. If your printer offers a scale, keep it at 100%.
  • Borderless isn't needed. Every photo sits more than 5 mm from the paper edge.
  • Each card should measure 75 × 100 mm after cutting. If yours come out smaller, the print was shrunk to fit; try printing borderless.
  • Save images stores the sheets in Photos for a print shop: 1800 × 1200 px JPEGs at 300 DPI.

Clear all sheets?

This removes every photo from the app. The originals in your library aren't touched.
